02 · Why now

For the grandmothers whose voices were edited out, and for the daughters not yet old enough to read.

Most of the women I come from did not get to speak. Their writing lived in margins and recipe cards. Their best lines were said at sinks, into the steam, to no one. Whole lifetimes of intelligence and feeling moved through them and left no public trace.

I think about this often. Especially when I am in a room of women, and the room is laughing or weeping or going very quiet, and someone has just said the thing that needed saying — and I realize how recent it is that we get to gather like this at all.

ALOUD is a stage and a community for women's voices, in writing and in speech, in performance and in private. But it is also, more quietly, an archive. Every word read aloud at ALOUD is recorded into the long memory of women's voices being heard.

It is a gift to the grandmothers we lost without ever fully hearing. It is a stage for the daughters who will inherit us. And it is, right now, an invitation to twenty-five women who want to help build it.

03 · What we're building

The architecture is a stack, not a list.

Three layers, working together. Events bring her in. Membership holds her. Care brings her back.

01
The Stage
Live · Public · Collective

Five formats of voice, each a different register. Spoken into a microphone. Written and read. Drafted in a circle of eight. Spoken over food. Voiced through the body.

02
The Room
Persistent · Digital · Communal

The membership layer. The community that lives between events — where members gather, share work in progress, find their pod, and build the archive-in-progress together.

03
The Care
Connective · Human · Intentional

The Check-In, and the ethos beneath everything. The reason a woman comes back. First-timers are sacred at ALOUD; this is the layer that holds the promise.

Each layer feeds the next. The stack is what makes ALOUD more than its events.

06 · A note from me

Sisterhood is the oldest technology I know.

I have spent the last two and a half years gathering people in writing circles in Lisbon. Over a hundred came through those rooms. Some of them are still my closest friends. All of them taught me that the room itself is the work — that what we build between us, in language and in attention, is real.

ALOUD is the next thing. It is the room I want to spend my life building. It is, specifically and intentionally, a room for women — because the lineage I keep finding myself inside is a lineage of women, and because the silence I keep finding myself wanting to break is the silence women have inherited.

If you are reading this, it is because I want you in it. Not the way a launch wants names on a list. The way a sister wants her sisters in the room when the room is being made.

The first 25 women to say yes will hold something nobody else can ever hold. I would be honoured if one of them were you.

— Genevieve Founder · ALOUD
07 · Questions, asked plainly

What you might be wondering, before you say yes.

Do I have to perform to be a founding member?+
No. Founding members are women who want this room to exist. You can listen for a year and never approach the mic. Your seat is your seat.
Is ALOUD only for women who write?+
Comedy. Music. Storytelling. Spoken word. Voice notes you've never read aloud. Five minutes, any format. The mic doesn't care what's on your CV.
What does "lifetime rate" actually mean?+
Whatever rate you lock in this month is the rate you pay for as long as you stay a member. Even ten years from now, in a chapter that doesn't yet exist. We will never raise it on you.
Can I bring a friend who isn't a member?+
Yes. Every founding member has a standing +1 for any Mic night, and a +2 for the first Mic on May 28. ALOUD doesn't grow without women bringing women.
What happens after the 25 are full?+
Membership stays open at the standard rate. The founding rate, founding gathering, and founding archive all close. The 25 names are sealed.
I don't live in Lisbon. Can I still join?+
Yes. Lisbon is the first chapter, not the only one. Founding members vote on city #2. The Room (digital) is global from day one.
